The archive downloaders select PLYs, camera files, and configuration from the source
archives using HTTP range requests, verify ZIP CRCs, and record SHA256 hashes.
Train uses the Voxel51 Gaussian model with the Graphdeco camera set.
The locomotion pipeline derives its simulation cage and Gaussian bindings locally.

Expected layout after downloading and preparing the assets:

```text
data/
  rainy_gs/fierygs/{Playground,firewoods_sand_dark}/
  rainy_gs/voxel51/train/
  gaussian_splashing/{bread,wolf,vasedeck}/
  enlivening_gs/{bread,wolf,vasedeck}/  # Generated by the locomotion asset adapter
  lava_gs/{zohar,lands_end,calico}/
  plant_wilting/source/{potted_plant,ficus,alocasia}/
  surface_tension/{food_apple_01,dry_quiver_leaf,wooden_bowl_02}/
  ice_melting/bunny.ply
```

Downloaded models and source data retain their upstream licenses and attribution.

Lava downloads use SuperSplat viewer manifests and the [PlayCanvas converter](https://github.com/playcanvas/splat-transform). Install the converter as described in [LavaGS](lava_gs/README.md). The selected LODs contain 2,999,888, 1,608,479, and 3,534,225 Gaussians respectively. Each prepared scene includes its source URL, author, CC BY 4.0 attribution, cameras, and downloaded-file hashes.

Water + Sand generates its scene geometry, materials, and particles procedurally.

Capillary Contact downloads 1K glTF scans from the Poly Haven API, checks upstream
MD5 sums, and records SHA256 hashes. Its paperclips, discs, stirrers, and basins
are procedural.

Ice Melting verifies the Stanford Bunny mesh against a pinned SHA-256. Its ice cubes, arch, basins, and studio are procedural.

Fire-X GS also needs the released FieryGS Gaussian-to-voxel mapping files.
Its downloader fetches each scene together with those files:

```bash
python -m firex_gs.fetch --scene all
```

Mappings are stored in `data/firex_gs/fierygs/`, Bonsai in
`data/firex_gs/graphdeco/bonsai/`, and generated material masks in
`data/firex_gs/prepared/`. [Scene preparation and run guide](firex_gs/README.md).

Multi-fluid generates its liquids, tea bag, glass stirrer, cup, and basins
procedurally. Run the three presets directly; see [the guide](implicit_mixture/README.md).

Ice Solidification generates its water particles, cold rod, stepped channel,
star mold, and studio procedurally. [Run guide](ice_solidification/README.md).

Rhizomorph generates its trees, foliage, soil cutaways, and rocks procedurally;
the three presets require no downloaded assets.

Windy Trees generates its mature source trees with the packaged Rhizomorph
presets, then records tree motion and SPH air trajectories. The inlet guide,
soil island, and studio are procedural. No downloaded assets are required;
completed Rhizomorph runs can be reused through `--source` in the
[method harness](windy_trees/README.md).

Magnetic Rigid Bodies generates its compass needles, electromagnetic crane,
beads, rigid triads, glass enclosure, and studio procedurally. No asset download
is needed. The showcased videos render the recorded Warp states with Blender;
paper PDFs and official reference videos are not bundled. [Run guide](magnetic_rigid/README.md).

Thin-Film Bubbles generates its geometric/material particles, studio apparatus,
lighting and optical materials procedurally. No asset download is required.
The three showcase videos render recorded Warp simulations with Blender Cycles;
the authors' source, paper PDF and reference videos are not bundled.
[Run guide](melp_thin_film/README.md) · [Sources](melp_thin_film/references/sources.json).

Robot Pouring downloads the 70 required Panda model files at a pinned Menagerie
revision, plus Google Sans Regular/Bold and its OFL notice. Every file is checked
against a bundled SHA256 manifest. The model XML and meshes remain unmodified
in `data/robot_pouring/franka_emika_panda/`; fonts live in
`data/robot_pouring/fonts/`. Both respect `PHYSSTUDIO_DATA_ROOT`. Cup, vessels,
plant and studio geometry are procedural. [Asset pins](robot_pouring/references/panda_assets.json) ·
[Font pins](robot_pouring/references/fonts.json).
